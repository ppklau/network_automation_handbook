# 02 — Business Alignment

## Purpose

Ensure every technical initiative in the handbook maps to measurable business value. Provide frameworks, templates, and language for connecting automation work to the outcomes that leadership cares about.

## Audience

- **Primary:** Network Infrastructure Leaders, Automation Architects
- **Secondary:** Engineers who need to justify their work to leadership

## Content Outline

### The Four Value Pillars
- Cost reduction
- Risk reduction
- Agility / speed
- Service quality

### Business Requirement → Technical Mapping
- How to trace a business need to a technical implementation
- Template: business requirement → design intent → implementation → KPI
- Worked example: ACME Investments trading latency requirement

### The "A-Team" Framework
- Why leadership, product management, architecture, and engineering alignment must precede tool selection
- Extends: [AI and Network Automation article](https://ppklau.github.io/article/ai_and_network_automation_for_finance_leaders/)
- Role definitions and responsibilities

### The Software Mindset
- Why software-centric thinking is a competitive advantage for infrastructure teams
- Extends: [What Anduril Teaches Us article](https://ppklau.github.io/article/what_anduril_teaches_us_about_software_mindset/)
- Learning speed as a differentiator

### Business Case Construction
- How to build a business case for network automation investment
- ROI model: initial investment vs compounding operational savings
- Risk framing: cost of inaction vs cost of transformation
- Template reference: [templates/executive/business-case.md](../templates/executive/)
