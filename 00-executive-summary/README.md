# 00 — Executive Summary

## Purpose

The executive summary provides the complete business case for network automation in a single, concise chapter. A senior leader should be able to read this chapter alone and understand why automation matters, what the transformation journey looks like, and how to measure success.

## Audience

- CIOs, CTOs, Heads of Network Infrastructure
- Non-technical stakeholders who approve investment

## Content Outline

### The Problem
- Manual network operations do not scale with business growth
- Configuration drift, human error, and slow change cycles create risk
- Compliance is retrospective and labour-intensive
- Network becomes a bottleneck rather than a business enabler

### The Opportunity
- Automation reduces MTTR, change failure rates, and operational cost
- One-touch deployment enables business scalability without proportional headcount growth
- Intent-based design creates auditable, traceable infrastructure
- The network becomes a platform that serves the business, not a constraint

### The Journey
- Five maturity levels: Reactive → Task-Based → Integrated Workflows → Network as Platform → Adaptive/Intent-Based
- Phased roadmap with measurable milestones at each stage
- Product thinking: treat automation as an internal product, not a one-off project

### The Investment
- People: skills evolution, hiring strategy, cross-functional teams
- Tooling: buy vs build decisions, orchestration platforms
- Process: CI/CD pipelines, testing frameworks, change management

### Measuring Success
- Executive dashboard: automation adoption %, MTTR reduction, change success rate, cost savings
- Engineering dashboard: pipeline success rate, test coverage, deployment frequency
- Operations dashboard: incident auto-resolution rate, drift detection
